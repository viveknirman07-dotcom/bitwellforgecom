# Rebuild the About experience

## Outcome
Replace the current About page with a new editorial narrative that explains what BitwellForge is, the commercial problem it addresses, how it thinks about interconnected growth systems, and the principles guiding its work.

## Page structure
1. Open with a concise institutional proposition and a restrained, contextual image.
2. Establish BitwellForge as a Revenue Infrastructure consulting firm for agencies, consultants, coaches, and B2B service businesses.
3. Contrast disconnected interventions with a systems view of commercial constraints, without diagrams or decorative graphics.
4. Explain the relationships between strategy, positioning, acquisition, revenue operations, execution, and durability through structured editorial text.
5. Close with three grounded working principles and a concise statement of intended value beyond the engagement.

## Visual direction
Use the homepage typography, colors, spacing logic, navigation, and footer as the source of truth. Build a precise twelve column editorial layout with controlled asymmetry, disciplined rules, selective existing people free imagery, and restrained reveal motion. Avoid cards, icons, decorative labels, oversized empty space, diagrams, and generic agency conventions.

## Content safeguards
Preserve established positioning and audience facts. Do not invent history, team members, client outcomes, credentials, statistics, or achievements. Keep the established BitwellForge wordmark treatment and the sitewide prohibition on dashes and bullets.

## Technical details
Replace the current About implementation cleanly rather than layering styles over it. Remove obsolete About interactions and animation code. Retain SEO metadata and structured data with language aligned to the rebuilt page. Verify the page at mobile, tablet, and desktop widths for text wrapping, overflow, image loading, reduced motion behavior, links, and console errors.
