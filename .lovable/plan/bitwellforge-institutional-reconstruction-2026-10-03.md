# BitwellForge institutional reconstruction

## Direction

Reconstruct the public BitwellForge experience as an original institutional publication and commercial operating system. Draw principles from leading strategy firms without copying layouts, imagery, language, typography, or proprietary brand elements. Preserve all working URLs, checkout, authentication, Forge Vault, affiliate attribution, entitlement, pricing, and post purchase flows.

## Visual system

Establish centralized semantic tokens around midnight, warm ivory, restrained gray, acid lime, and cobalt. Replace the current luxury serif treatment with a contemporary grotesk led hierarchy, neutral professional body typography, and selective technical labels. Use a twelve column editorial grid, thin structural rules, sharp or subtly rounded surfaces, deliberate asymmetry, and recurring system line, numerical label, indicator, and modular block motifs.

## Public experience

### Navigation and footer

Build a compact sticky institutional header with keyboard accessible capability navigation, clear active states, a composed full screen mobile menu, and direct routes to conversation and capabilities. Rebuild the footer as a large structured index with capabilities, insights, company, careers, contact, social, legal, and correct business and support contact roles.

### Homepage

Create a progressive authority journey: editorial system hero, strategy and systems positioning strip, fragmented growth problem, signature BitwellForge model, interactive capability explorer, technology and AI architecture, five stage engagement model, verified case study presentation, editorial insights, featured thinking, human practice section, and a restrained final conversation invitation. Preserve factual content and never invent outcomes, clients, people, or testimonials.

### Core public pages

Recompose Services, service details, Process, Case Studies, case study details, Insights, insight articles, About, Contact, and Careers into the same editorial system. Contact becomes an institutional intake experience while retaining existing submission behavior and anti spam safeguards. Forge Vault marketing pages adopt the public visual language without altering pricing or purchase logic.

## Interaction and responsive behavior

Use restrained transform and opacity motion, progressive system construction, editorial reveals, purposeful sticky moments, and reduced motion fallbacks. Recompose key experiences for 320 pixel mobile, tablet, desktop, and wide desktop rather than merely stacking desktop sections. Maintain 44 pixel targets, visible focus, semantic structure, keyboard operation, readable line lengths, and no horizontal overflow.

## Technical approach

Create reusable public experience components and data driven content modules while leaving protected product and backend boundaries intact. Consolidate tokens in the global design system, keep route level SEO and canonical behavior, preserve the exact favicon, and retain the current production metadata unless a page specific title is required. Use optimized local or generated visual assets only where they communicate people, architecture, technology, or systems.

## Verification

Validate every public route and protected handoff in dark and light contexts at phone, tablet, desktop, and wide desktop sizes. Check navigation, forms, search and filters, keyboard behavior, reduced motion, overflow, console and runtime errors, metadata, sitemap and robots coverage, and the production build. Do not publish without an explicit publishing request.
